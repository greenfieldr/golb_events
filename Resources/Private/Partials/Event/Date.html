Renders the date and time of an occurrence as elements: the day (or the range of days) for all day occurrences, otherwise start and end with their times. Times are shown in the server time zone. Variables: occurrence (Occurrence), settings (plugin settings with dateFormat and timeFormat). {occurrence.start -> f:format.date(format: settings.dateFormat)} – {occurrence.end -> f:format.date(format: settings.dateFormat)} {occurrence.start -> f:format.date(format: settings.dateFormat)} {occurrence.start -> f:format.date(format: settings.timeFormat)} – {occurrence.end -> f:format.date(format: settings.dateFormat)} {occurrence.end -> f:format.date(format: settings.timeFormat)}
